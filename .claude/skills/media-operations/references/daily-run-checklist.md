# Daily Run Checklist

## Intelligence
- [ ] Trend brief records freshness, momentum, saturation and confidence.
- [ ] Competitor observations use approved public sources only.
- [ ] Content gap is original and does not copy protected creative work.

## Discovery
- [ ] Gather candidates from multiple categories.
- [ ] Check freshness and publication dates.
- [ ] Score candidates consistently.
- [ ] Avoid repetitive topics.

## Research
- [ ] Build source ledger.
- [ ] Verify names, dates, numbers and quotations.
- [ ] Distinguish fact, estimate and opinion.
- [ ] Remove unsupported claims.

## Creative direction and audience
- [ ] Creative brief defines audience, takeaway, format and visual concept.
- [ ] Audience insights distinguish observed data from assumptions.
- [ ] Accessibility and mobile readability are checked.

## Production
- [ ] Script timed to approximately 30 seconds.
- [ ] Poster generated and proofread.
- [ ] Video rendered and probed.
- [ ] Preview frames inspected.
- [ ] Captions checked and synced.
- [ ] Asset rights documented.

## Publishing
- [ ] Platform-specific copy complete.
- [ ] Correct account and permissions confirmed.
- [ ] Quality gates passed.
- [ ] Duplicate check passed.
- [ ] Publish result verified per platform.
- [ ] Canonical post URLs recorded.

## Growth, revenue and operations
- [ ] Platform metrics retain their definitions and date ranges.
- [ ] Growth recommendations are evidence-based; no artificial engagement.
- [ ] Revenue research has fit/conflict/disclosure notes; no unauthorised outreach.
- [ ] Costs, quotas, failures, backups and kill-switch status are recorded.

## Reporting
- [ ] Daily report saved.
- [ ] Errors and retries recorded.
- [ ] No unsupported claim of completion.
- [ ] Escalate high-risk or blocked items.
