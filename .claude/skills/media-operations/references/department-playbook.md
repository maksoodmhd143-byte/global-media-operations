# Department Playbook

This document translates the eight added departments into repeatable work products. Treat these as implementation requirements, not claims that the software already exists.

## Trend Intelligence
**Output:** `trend-brief.md` with candidate, source, first-seen date, freshness, momentum evidence, saturation, shelf life, geography, confidence and recommended action.
**Pass condition:** each selected trend has verifiable evidence and is not based solely on a search snippet or viral post.

## Competitor Intelligence
**Output:** `competitor-observations.csv` and `content-gap.md`. Track only approved public accounts and publicly visible information. Record URLs and observation dates. Never copy scripts, visual identity or protected assets.
**Pass condition:** the proposed opportunity is an original angle and observations are not misrepresented as private analytics.

## Creative Director
**Output:** `creative-brief.md` specifying audience, takeaway, concept, format, hook, scene rhythm, visual language, accessibility and platform adaptations.
**Pass condition:** concept serves the evidence and does not exaggerate the story.

## Multimedia Studio
**Output:** editable project files, original graphics, licensed media manifest, narration/transcript, captions, final exports and validation report.
**Pass condition:** files exist, open correctly and pass technical, rights and visual inspection.

## Audience Intelligence
**Output:** `audience-insights.md` with observed themes, data source, sample size where known, confidence, limitations and testable hypotheses.
**Pass condition:** no sensitive personal profiling and no unsupported claims about the whole audience from a small sample.

## Growth Manager
**Output:** `growth-report.md` with metric definitions, platform, date range, sample size, comparison baseline, experiment and recommendation.
**Pass condition:** platform metrics are not conflated and conclusions are proportionate to evidence.

## Revenue Intelligence
**Output:** `revenue-opportunities.md` with opportunity type, public source, brand fit, eligibility, conflicts, disclosure needs and approval status.
**Pass condition:** research only unless explicit permission is granted for outreach, applications, spending or agreements. Never promise income.

## Operations Manager
**Output:** `operations-report.md` with run status, scheduled jobs, failures, retries, costs, quotas, storage, backup status, credential expiry status (never token values), and kill-switch status.
**Pass condition:** failures are visible, retries are safe, secrets are protected and the stop procedure works.

## Department handoff rules
1. Trend and competitor desks provide evidence; they do not decide factual truth.
2. Editorial owns factual claims and corrections.
3. Creative direction cannot alter facts to improve engagement.
4. Multimedia uses only documented rights-cleared assets.
5. Quality control can block any package.
6. Distribution cannot bypass a block or publish to an unverified account.
7. Growth and audience insights inform future choices but cannot override editorial standards.
8. Revenue cannot dictate editorial conclusions or initiate external commitments without approval.
9. Operations can pause any workflow for security, cost, reliability or policy concerns.


## Community Management
**Output:** `community-management-report.md`, a versioned brand knowledge base, approved response playbooks, conversation audit records and human-review queue.
**Scope:** 24/7 monitoring of authorised and API-supported messages, comments, mentions and notifications on YouTube, Instagram, LinkedIn and Facebook. Coverage is platform- and permission-specific; do not imply universal inbox access.
**Pass condition:** every inbound event is deduplicated, classified and risk-scored; only allowlisted low-risk replies are sent automatically; uncertain, sensitive or commitment-making replies are escalated; every action is auditable; pause and token-revocation controls work.

### Training data structure
Maintain editable, version-controlled files or database records for brand facts, FAQs, approved replies, rejected examples, tone rules, escalation rules, prohibited promises and platform-specific response policies. Store source/approval metadata and effective dates. Do not treat a user correction as approved policy until saved as an approved revision.
