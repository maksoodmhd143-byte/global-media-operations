# Editorial Policy

## Editorial promise
Publish useful, accurate, understandable content for a worldwide English-speaking audience.

## Source hierarchy
1. Primary documents, official datasets, research papers, court/regulator/company statements.
2. Reputable independent journalism and established subject-matter organisations.
3. Specialist commentary, used as context rather than proof.

Do not treat search snippets, social posts, anonymous claims or AI-generated summaries as sufficient evidence for material claims.

## Claim ledger
For each material claim, record:
- Claim ID
- Exact claim
- Source URL
- Publisher
- Publication date
- Primary/secondary/commentary classification
- Supporting excerpt or data location
- Verification status
- Confidence
- Script scene using the claim

## Editorial risk
Require human review for medical, legal, financial, election, conflict, disaster, criminal allegation, privacy and high-impact personal claims. Do not publish unverified allegations about identifiable people or organisations.

## Corrections
If a published item is materially wrong:
1. Stop scheduled derivatives.
2. Record the error and evidence.
3. Correct, update or remove the post where platform tools permit.
4. Publish a correction where appropriate.
5. Record the incident and prevention action.
